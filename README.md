Blunder - a chess puzzle game(in the making)!!!

Description:
This is the prototype of a chess logic/puzzle game built in Godot! It is going to have all sorts of logic games embedded in Chess, and current ideas include a chess soduku, a chess minesweeper, a chess battleship, and much more!!! It is aimed at people with an rough idea of how chess pieces move, but beginner-friendly functionalities will be added further on. 

For now, it functions as a normal chess board where different pieces can move like they are supposed to, there's alternating turns for black and white, and the King can't move in illegal places. 

This game was brought to existence after a midnight chess frenzy that diminished 100 elos ;-;

How it was made:
This game is created on Godot. It was created by using the board as a vector to arrange the pieces. 

How to Play it:
Drag the pieces around with your mouse! They should move correctly. White goes first and then Black moves. You cannot castle or en passant yet, but you can capture pieces and put the king in check!
